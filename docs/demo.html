▶ What the platform core is

Registry

Loads plugins against a manifest (name/version/capabilities). Overclaims are NAMED refusals, never silent. Every load and call is receipted with content hashes.

Session

Agent proposals append to a WAL. Effects are held until evidence is durable and the verdict passes. Evidence-present is necessary, never sufficient.

verify()

Offline re-derivation: replay the WAL's recorded args through the plugin; any verdict that doesn't re-derive is flagged — the wal_ref-substitution defense.

quilt_vote

Demo plugin: 2-bit ternary ballot (Reject 0b00 / Abstain 0b01 / Agree 0b10), two passes, warp=32. Deterministic; pure python. One warp = one ballot.

▶ Why this is the rebuild

The old monolith tried to be the whole environment. The platform bet is the fleet bet: thin host, plugin constellation, receipts everywhere. warp-vote, ternary-warp, flux, cudaclaw, holodeck, the room family — each becomes a manifest + capabilities + pins, loaded into this host.
